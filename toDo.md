


Suggestions
Anti-trampas — the score is posted from the browser, so a student who opens devtools can POST a fake pleno. Fix: the server hands out the questions and validates the answers. Worth doing before medals mean anything socially.
Medals with tiers — right now it's binary. Bronce/plata/oro at 1/5/15 plenos per mode would keep people playing after the first one.
Rachas — "3 días seguidos practicando" is the single most effective mechanic in this kind of app, and you already have the timestamps to compute it.
Notas are browser-local — change computer and they're gone. Say the word and I'll add a student_notes table back (I removed it because nothing used it).
Elegir número de preguntas (12 / 24 / 48) — with medals only from 24 up, so short sessions stay useful without cheapening the achievement.
Rockschool y Constructor de melodías don't score — they're the only two modes with no feedback loop, so they feel like dead ends next to the others.
